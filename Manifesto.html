I. The Acquisition Paradigm

The core engine of J & B Autos is absolute margin control. The vehicle arbitrage corridor running from the southern I-24/I-65 hubs up to Wisconsin is highly lucrative, but only if the acquisition mathematics are flawless. Every purchase is governed by the Accountability Matrix.

When to use AutoBidMaster (ABM)

The Protocol: Use ABM for all current acquisitions. ABM is a Copart-licensed broker. As an unlicensed entity, ABM acts as our armor.

The Math: Copart penalizes unlicensed public buyers with a massive escalating fee structure. ABM shields the operation from this by utilizing their master dealer license. We pay ABM a capped transaction fee ($299 or 5%, whichever is greater) and inherit the lower, native Copart dealer fees. This opens access to dealer-only inventory and protects the margin on every vehicle.

When to use Copart Direct

The Protocol: Transition to Copart Direct only after securing a State Dealer License.

The Math: Copart is the source. Buying direct removes the middleman, but doing so without a license bleeds capital. Once licensed, you bypass the ABM transaction fee entirely and pay native wholesale fees directly to Copart. Copart Direct is the endgame for maximum margin, locked behind state licensing.

II. The Scaling Protocol

Scaling requires extreme discipline. J & B Autos will not scale by acquiring random vehicles; the operation will scale by upgrading licensing, tightening logistics, and dominating specific inventory classes.

  • Phase 1: The Broker Shield. Execute exclusively through the ABM Advanced tier. Target unclassified fleet vehicles, theft recoveries, and hail damage units. Batch purchases in Nashville and Louisville to consolidate 3-car or 4-car hauler freight costs up to Wisconsin.
  • Phase 2: The Licensing Pivot. Allocate accumulated capital to secure a commercial location that satisfies state requirements. Apply for and secure the official Dealer License. Sever the AutoBidMaster account and register directly with Copart as a licensed dealer.
  • Phase 3: High-Volume Arbitrage. Utilize the dealer license to bid directly on bank repossessions and high-tier dealer-only trades. Establish dedicated freight contracts moving from Tennessee to the upper Midwest.

Red Team Warning: Do not miss payment windows. The vehicle amount must be paid within 3 business days of purchase to avoid a $50.00 late fee per vehicle. If not paid within 8 calendar days, the vehicle will be relisted, triggering a penalty of 10% of the final sale price (minimum $600).
